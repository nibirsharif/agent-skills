FR-001: When a gateway sends a reading whose production value is more than the rated capacity of the solar system, the monitoring service shall discard the reading.

FR-002: Where a homeowner has turned on quiet hours, the alert service shall hold every alert except Offline alerts until quiet hours end.

FR-003: When a solar system is offline, the mobile app shall display a friendly message explaining the problem.

FR-004: When the production of a day is less than 50% of the forecast, the alert service shall notify the homeowner by push or email.

FR-005: The alert service shall send alert emails within 15 minutes of the event that triggers them.

Q-001: How long must the gateway buffer readings? Proposed: 24 hours.

Q-002: What is the alert limit? Proposed: none.
