FR-001: When a guest submits the booking form, the booking service shall send a confirmation email to the email address of that guest.

FR-002: When a guest cancels a booking, the booking service shall mark the room of that booking as available for the dates of that booking.

NFR-001: When a guest submits the booking form, the booking service shall send a confirmation email to the email address of that guest within 2 minutes of the submission.
