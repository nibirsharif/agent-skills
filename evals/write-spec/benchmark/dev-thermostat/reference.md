Set: dev. Domain: device. Form: statements.

Open decisions:
- R1: Whether a setpoint of exactly 5 °C and exactly 30 °C is allowed ("between 5 and 30").
- R2: What the thermostat controller does when the temperature sensor gives no reading or fails.
- R3: What the thermostat controller does with the heating relay when a window opens while the relay is on, or closes while the room is below the setpoint.
- R4: What the thermostat controller does when the user changes the setpoint while the relay is on or off (for example to below the room temperature).

Not given (must not appear in a requirement): any value other than 0.5 °C, 5 °C, 30 °C; any behaviour on sensor failure.
