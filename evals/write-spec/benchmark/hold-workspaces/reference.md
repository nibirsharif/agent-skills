Set: holdout. Domain: data and permissions. Form: prose.

Open decisions:
- R1: What the notes service does when an invite is opened more than 72 hours after it was sent.
- R2: What the notes service does when an admin invites an email address that is already a member.
- R3: What the notes service does when a member who is not an admin tries to delete a note.
- R4: What "oldest member" means (joined first, or age), and what happens when the last admin leaves and no member remains.
- R5: What "leaves" means as an event the notes service can observe.

Not given: a response to an expired invite, a response to a refused delete, a tie-break rule.
