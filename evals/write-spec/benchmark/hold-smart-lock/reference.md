Set: holdout. Domain: device. Form: prose.

Open decisions:
- R1: What the firmware does when the handle is tapped and no registered phone is within 2 metres.
- R2: What the firmware does when the door is still open 30 seconds after unlocking, and when it then locks.
- R3: How the user is warned of low battery (which channel or signal).
- R4: Whether the warning is given once when the battery reaches 20%, or repeatedly at or below 20%.
- R5: Whether the mechanical key statement requires anything of the firmware.

Not given: a warning channel, a behaviour for an open door, a behaviour for an unregistered phone.
